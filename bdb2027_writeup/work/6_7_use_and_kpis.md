# Stages 6-7: How a team uses it (adapted from roadmap/KPIs)

Pre-draft (scouting): rank speed from the 40 (tracked S0 adds nothing over the official time);
treat drill-based acceleration/braking as low-weight evidence.
Weeks 1-5: blend the Combine top-speed prior with game tracking; switch acceleration and braking to
game tracking from Week 1.
Weekly (coaching/performance): Engine Card per rookie, refreshed after each game:
- engine (A0, S0, braking) and share of Combine S0 used (position medians 82-93%);
- launch U by week against his own norm; flag weeks >= 3 points below.
KPIs to monitor: weekly launch U (rushers), share of Combine S0 used (WR/DB), engine stability
(game-to-game change in A0/S0).
