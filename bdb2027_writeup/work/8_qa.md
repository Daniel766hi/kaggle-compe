# Stage 8: Likely judge questions

1. Why fit S0 beyond 40 yards? Mono-exponential extrapolation is standard (Samozino 2016); S0 test-retest .96.
2. Why is in-game A0 higher than Combine A0? Moving starts vs three-point stance; we never subtract them.
3. Is "worth 5 games" precise? No: 90% interval 2.2 to >8; the acceleration result (<1 game) is tight.
4. Why within position and weight? Otherwise WR-vs-OL differences dominate (position+weight R^2 0.66-0.79).
5. Does launch U just mean the play was a pass rush vs drop? Only plays with a recorded get-off; player FE; role control.
6. Reverse causality for pressure? Launch window is the first second; pressures inside it are excluded.
7. Why does OL show the opposite sign? Hard launches by linemen signal stress (recovering, being beaten).
8. Why no DB outcome? No per-snap coverage outcome for rookies in the data; DBs appear in carry-over only.
9. Why not use the `a` column? It is a magnitude; braking would count as acceleration.
10. Glitches? Second-best envelope, |a| > 12 yd/s^2 removed, fits with R^2 < .5 or S0 > 13.5 rejected.
11. Multiple testing? BH-FDR across 24 player-level tests.
12. Selection (who plays)? Analyses condition on players with tracked games; stated as a limitation.
13. Draft capital? Controlled (log pick) in outcome tests.
14. Can teams compute it weekly? Yes: needs only each player's own tracking; notebook runs in minutes.
15. Is Will Anderson cherry-picked? He is selected automatically as the rookie with most tracked rushes.
