About Vision Lab

Vision Lab is a free, hands-on way to see how computer vision works: point a camera at the world and watch a neural network find, follow and count objects, then take the same picture apart with the classic image-processing tools that factory and inspection cameras still rely on.

It was built by an Industrial Engineering graduate of Universitas Gadjah Mada, Indonesia, as a side project in data analysis and computer vision. The line counter comes from an operations question: how would you count people through a doorway or parts along a conveyor without buying special hardware?

What it is built with

  • TensorFlow.js and the COCO-SSD model (Apache 2.0), running in your browser.
  • jsQR (Apache 2.0) for QR codes.
  • Plain JavaScript for the tracker, motion map, k-means palette, filters, edge detection, colour measurement and histogram.
  • Hosted for free on GitHub Pages.
