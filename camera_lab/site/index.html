See what a neural network sees

Turn on your camera or pick a photo. Vision Lab finds people, animals and 80 kinds of everyday objects, counts them crossing a line, and takes the image apart with classic image processing. Everything runs on your device; nothing is uploaded.

Colour segmentation

Measure a colour

Tap the image to pick a colour. Everything within the tolerance stays in colour, the rest turns grey, and its share of the image is measured, the way a line camera checks ripeness, rust or a missing label. With the camera on, the share updates live.

Things to try

Detect

Point it at your desk

Cups, bottles, phones, laptops, keyboards and books are all in the 80 classes. Lower the confidence to see what the network is unsure about.

Count

Count a doorway

Choose People, put the line across the door and leave the phone on a shelf. Each person is counted once, in the direction they cross.

Colour

Measure ripeness

Tap a banana or a leaf and watch the share of that colour. The same idea checks fruit, rust or labels on a factory line.

Exposure

Read a histogram

Aim at a window, then at a dark corner, and see the pixels pile up at the bright or dark end.
