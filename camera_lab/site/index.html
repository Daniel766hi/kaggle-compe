k-means clustering

Main colours

Pixels are grouped into k clusters by colour; each cluster's average is one palette colour, sized by its share of the picture. Tap a colour to copy its code.

    Measure a colour

    Tap the image to pick a colour. Everything within the tolerance stays in colour, the rest turns grey, and its share of the image is measured, the way a line camera checks ripeness, rust or a missing label. With the camera on, the share updates live.

    Things to try

    Detect & count

    Count a doorway

    Choose People, put the line across the door and leave the phone on a shelf. Each person is counted once, in the direction they cross, and the log downloads as a spreadsheet.

    Privacy blur

    Share a street photo

    Open a photo of a crowded place and let the detector pixelate every person, or every number plate area, before you post it.

    Motion map

    Find the busy spot

    Leave the camera on a room or a shop shelf for a minute and the heat map shows where people actually walk and reach.

    QR scanner

    Scan a code safely

    See where a QR code points before you open it. Try the QR sample photo above.

    Colour palette

    Steal a colour scheme

    Point at a painting, a poster or a sunset and copy its main colours as hex codes for your slides or designs.

    Colour measure

    Measure ripeness

    Tap a banana or a leaf and watch the share of that colour. The same idea checks fruit, rust or labels on a factory line.
