Privacy policy

Your camera and photos stay on your device

When you start the camera or open a photo, the picture is processed entirely inside your browser. It is not uploaded, stored or sent to this site or anyone else. Stopping the camera, closing the tab or choosing another photo discards it. The browser asks your permission before the camera turns on, and you can withdraw it at any time in your browser's site settings.

"Save this image", "Record clip" and "Download log (CSV)" write files to your own device; nothing is sent anywhere. QR codes are decoded on your device too, and links in them are never opened for you.

What the site loads from other services

Like most websites, loading this one makes your browser contact a few services, which receive your IP address and browser details as part of any normal web request:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site and may keep server logs (GitHub privacy statement).
  • jsDelivr delivers the TensorFlow.js libraries (jsDelivr privacy policy).
  • Google Fonts delivers the typefaces (Google privacy policy).

The site itself sets no cookies and runs no analytics of its own.

Advertising

This site may show ads served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google's Ad Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at www.aboutads.info. To learn more, see how Google uses cookies in advertising and how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it. Questions can be raised on the site's GitHub repository.
