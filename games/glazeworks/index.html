Glazeworks

Fire glazed tiles on an 8×8 kiln shelf. Fill a row or column and it fires off the shelf.

  • Pure lines score ×3. Clear a line in one glaze colour and the kiln heats up.
  • Heat earns turns. Every 3 heat gives a ↻ token. Tap a tile in the tray to rotate it.
  • Keep firing. Four placements without a clear and a cracked shard lands on the shelf.
