## Theme 2.1 — School feeding / public procurement and food markets

- Handbury, J. & Moshary, S. — *School Food Policy Affects Everyone: Retail Responses to the National School Lunch Program*, NBER Working Paper w29384.
  [NBER page](https://www.nber.org/papers/w29384) — large US programme moved retail prices; useful international comparison.
- Ghana home-grown school feeding impact evaluation (CGIAR) — local market effects depend on procurement scale and market efficiency.
  [CGSpace PDF](https://cgspace.cgiar.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/c7cf6219-6586-4338-8778-2a676757eabf/content)
- HGSF Working Paper 13 — *Supply chain analysis of different school feeding models*.
  [HGSF resource bank](https://hgsf-global.org/en/bank/downloads/doc_details/452-hgsf-working-paper-13-supply-chain-analysis-of-different-school-feeding-models)
- MBG procurement in South Tangerang (intermediaries dominate) — IPB *Jurnal Manajemen & Organisasi*.
  [IPB journal](https://journal.ipb.ac.id/jmo/article/view/73792)
- Local food for MBG in Central Java (closed vs open supplier systems).
  [Jateng journal](https://ejournal.jatengprov.go.id/index.php/AKD/article/view/1509)
- **To search yourself:** Brazil PNAE (30% family-farm procurement law), India PM-POSHAN, WFP home-grown school feeding.

## Theme 2.2 — Demand shocks, biological lags, commodity cycles

- Meadows, D. L. (1970). *Dynamics of Commodity Production Cycles.* Wright-Allen Press. (classic SD commodity cycle)
- Mitchell, Thompson & Malone (2024) — biological lags and US egg prices during HPAI 2022–23 (distributed lag, ~5.3% weekly retail effect).
  [IDEAS/RePEc](https://ideas.repec.org/a/ags/jlaare/302455.html)
- Endogenous price fluctuations in Pakistan's chicken supply chain; price transmission in poultry.
  [KFUPM – endogenous fluctuations](https://pure.kfupm.edu.sa/en/publications/endogenous-price-fluctuations-evidence-from-the-chicken-supply-ch/) ·
  [KFUPM – price transmission](https://pure.kfupm.edu.sa/en/publications/price-transmission-in-pakistans-poultry-supply-chain/)
- Sterman, J. D. (2000). *Business Dynamics*, ch. 17–20 (supply line, commodity cycles, oscillation).

### Gap check, 10 November 2026: closely related MBG studies to read and consider citing

A web search found no study that models MBG demand and egg/chicken prices with system dynamics
or scenario discovery. These studies are close and reviewers may know them. Their details could
not be opened from the cloud session (sites blocked), so verify authors, year and venue yourself.

- ARIMA model of egg and milk prices after the MBG launch in North Sumatra (statistical; reports a
  rise in average egg prices and volatility after MBG). A statistical counterpart to this paper's
  counterfactual: cite it in Related Work and contrast the methods.
  https://journal.itisd.org/index.php/sisfotekjar/article/view/186
- System dynamics model of the egg supply chain in Bojonegoro (Vensim; production, distribution,
  market price; no programme demand). Belongs with Section 2.2.
  https://www.researchgate.net/publication/390737456_Model_Sistem_Dinamis_Dalam_Meningkatkan_Efisiensi_Rantai_Pasok_Telur_Ayam_Di_Bojonegoro
- Multi-commodity inventory model (EOQ, JIT, reorder point) for MBG kitchens, Gunung Sindur.
  Operational, kitchen-level; contrasts with this paper's market-level view.
  https://www.researchgate.net/publication/413956512_Multi-Commodity_Inventory_Model_for_Free_Nutritious_Food_Programs_Based_on_Economic_Order_Quantity_Just-in-Time_and_Reorder_Point_A_Case_Study_of_Gunung_Sindur_District
