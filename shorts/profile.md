# Video channel profile: ridiculous animals

The TikTok and Instagram Reels agents plan every video from this file (the X agent keeps using
`x_agent/profile.md`). Edit it to change the channel; set TIKTOK_PROFILE or INSTAGRAM_PROFILE to
give a platform its own file.

## The channel

Short comedy clips of animals in funny, ridiculous moments (free stock footage by default, or AI
clips). The joke is always the animal: its absurd confidence, its dramatic overreaction, or a very
human situation it handles badly. Goal: something people watch twice and send to a friend.

## Format: a three-beat sketch, one beat per shot

1. **Setup**: the animal in an ordinary situation. The on-screen text sets up the joke.
2. **Escalation**: it gets weirder or more serious than it should.
3. **Punchline**: the ridiculous payoff, a reveal, or a deadpan reaction.

Each shot is one short clip, so every beat must read as a single clear moment with a single clear
action.

## Ideas to mix

- Animals doing human jobs badly: a cat as a strict office manager, a goose as a security guard,
  a pug as a barista who only makes one drink.
- Dramatic overreactions to tiny things: a dog betrayed by a cucumber, a hamster in a slow-motion
  action-movie escape from a sock.
- Absurd confidence: a chicken that believes it is an eagle, a tiny kitten "guarding" the house.
- Unlikely duos: a duck and a capybara running a business together.
- Everyday Indonesian scenes, played by animals: a goat queuing at a warung, a cat supervising a
  nasi goreng cart, an orangutan stuck in Jakarta traffic.
- Animal versions of familiar formats: job interviews, cooking shows, nature-documentary narration
  of something mundane, "POV" and "me when..." memes.

## Rules

- Clearly fiction: never present a clip as a real event or imitate real news footage.
- Animals are never hurt, scared, in danger, distressed or mistreated, and nothing shows a stunt
  someone might copy with a real pet.
- No real people, celebrities, brands or logos.
- Family-friendly: no gore, nothing sexual, no jokes at the expense of groups of people.
- Look: bright and clear, the animal large and central, close-ups.

## Voice

- English, simple and punchy. Bahasa Indonesia is fine for a joke aimed at Indonesians.
- On-screen text is deadpan and meme-like: a few words per shot, with the punchline in the last one.
- Caption: one funny line plus 3 to 5 hashtags, including #funnyanimals.
